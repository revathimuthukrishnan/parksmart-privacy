ParkSmart Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Designa Access Corporation ("Designa", "we", "us") collects, uses, and handles information when you use the ParkSmart skill on Amazon Alexa.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the ParkSmart Alexa skill, we collect only the information necessary to process your parking reservation:

  • Location (city or parking facility name)
  • Requested parking start and end dates and times
  • Booking reference numbers (for cancellations or viewing reservations)

We do not collect your name, email address, payment information, or any other personal data through the Alexa skill.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used solely to:

  • Search for available parking spots at your requested location and time
  • Create, view, or cancel parking reservations in Designa's reservation system

We do not use your information for marketing, profiling, or any purpose other than fulfilling your parking reservation request.

3. Data Retention

Session data collected during your interaction with the ParkSmart skill is temporary. Once your reservation is completed or your session ends, session data is cleared and not retained by the skill.

Reservation data (booking reference, dates, location) is stored in Designa's reservation system as part of your booking record, subject to Designa's general data retention policies.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Your reservation data is processed exclusively within Designa's internal reservation system.

5. Amazon Alexa

The ParkSmart skill operates on the Amazon Alexa platform. Your use of Alexa is also subject to Amazon's Privacy Policy, available at https://www.amazon.com/privacy. Designa is not responsible for data collected by Amazon through the Alexa platform.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information processed through the ParkSmart skill. All communication between the skill and Designa's reservation system is encrypted using HTTPS.

7. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection law (including GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data held by Designa. To exercise these rights, please contact us at the address below.
